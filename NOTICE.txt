Arabic text, transliteration and English translation in this app

Arabic text (Uthmani script) and the transliteration are from the Tanzil
Project, https://tanzil.net, licensed under the Creative Commons
Attribution 3.0 license (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).
Unmodified.

The English translation is Mohammed Marmaduke Pickthall's "The Meaning
of the Glorious Qur'an" (1930). It is in the public domain (UK since
2007; US since January 1, 2026, per the 95-year rule for works
published before 1930). Quoted unchanged.

This app, Ayah, is an independent reading tool. It is not affiliated
with the Tanzil Project, Pickthall's estate, Versling, or any other
Quran app.

The underlying ayah data (Arabic / transliteration / translation /
surah metadata) was ported verbatim from the already-cleared dataset
shipped in the sibling product Versling (~/products/versling,
public/data/surah-*.json, public/data/surahs.json), which sourced it
the same way described above. No content was re-derived or fabricated.
