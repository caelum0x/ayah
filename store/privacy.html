Ayah privacy

Ayah is an iPhone app for reading the Quran one ayah (verse) at a time. The Arabic text and transliteration are from the Tanzil Project (CC BY 3.0, tanzil.net). The English translation is M. M. Pickthall's 1930 translation, public domain, quoted unchanged.

Kept ayat and private notes are stored only on the phone, in the app's local storage. Ayah has no account, no server, and no analytics. It does not sell or share personal information because it does not collect any.

The Share button uses the iOS share sheet. What happens after that is controlled by the app you choose.

The app is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
